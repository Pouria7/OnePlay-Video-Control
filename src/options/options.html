Keyboard Shortcuts

Customize global video playback shortcuts and combination keys (e.g. Space, Ctrl+M, Alt+Right).

Action Description Shortcut Key
Play / Pause Toggle playback of the active video
Seek Backward Jump backward by configured seek interval (-5s)
Seek Forward Jump forward by configured seek interval (+5s)
Volume Up Increase playback volume by 10%
Volume Down Decrease playback volume by 10%
Mute / Unmute Toggle audio mute / unmute
Toggle Fullscreen Enter or exit full-screen mode

💡 Tip: Click any shortcut badge above and press your desired key or combination (e.g. Ctrl + M, Alt + Right, Shift + Space). Press Esc to cancel.
